Drawer¶

class KXDrawer¶

A drawer widget that can be opened and closed by tapping on its tab or programmatically.

anchor: Literal['lt', 'lm', 'lb', 'rt', 'rm', 'rb', 'bl', 'bm', 'br', 'tl', 'tm', 'tr'] = 'lm'¶

Where the drawer comes out from.

anim_duration = 0.2¶

The time taken for the drawer to open and close. This does not include the time taken for the triangle on the tab to rotate, which is also anim_duration.

bg_color = '#222222'¶

Background color

close(*_unused)¶

Closes the drawer if it’s open

fg_color = '#AAAAAA'¶

Foreground color. Currently, it is only used for the color of the triangle on the tab.
